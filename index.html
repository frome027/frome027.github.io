Education📚

My name is Fatima Romero, and I am from Riverside, California. I am a full-time student at the University of California, Riverside. I am in my fourth year, double majoring in Education and Liberal Arts, and will graduate in June 2027. I have gained various work experiences that have strengthened my skills. These roles included customer service, loan representative, shipping clerk, mortgage representative, client recruiter, and more. These roles gave me experience working with others, serving companies, and meeting their needs through customer satisfaction and meeting goals. I gained a lot of knowledge about policies and procedures during my time in those roles. I learned that my role mattered in making an ordinary experience special. I learned how to deliver service while staying flexible and adjusting to a client's persona.

I made a complete change when I returned to school in 2022 to pursue a career in Education. It was a drastic change, going from black-and-white procedures to the opposite: learning that there is more than one way to approach obstacles. Since switching careers, I have held many roles in Education that have shaped my experience as an Educator. I started as a program leader in an after-school program where academics and fun were blended into the time we spent there. I learned hands-on learning, curriculum creation, rule implementation, structure, and leading a class of Kindergarteners with one other leader. In this setting, I saw the side most teachers do not get to see: what it is like for students who attend school all day and then have to stay on campus for another 4 hours because staff needs to care for them due to parents' schedules. It helped me understand how school fatigue or frustration can affect students' behavior and carry into the next day in the classroom. From there,  I became a student teacher's aide at Riverside Community College, where I helped kindergarten students with language arts and guided them through levels of mastery. I ran centers from time to time and got to see what the classroom culture was like.  In my most recent role, I worked as a Kindergarten teacher's aide, specializing in one-on-one support for students who needed it.  In this role, I witnessed the different levels of students in one grade. Although I was in a kindergarten class, the students were at very different levels, and I had to actively keep them engaged while meeting the needs of those who needed additional care.

I look forward to taking the experiences I have had and applying them in the classroom. I am eager to apply what I have learned and make each student's experience the best one. I know through my education and past careers that we have to act in the best interest of the student, which means being flexible, structured, and understanding.
